Duel（以下称「我们」）是一款和朋友在生活习惯上对决的 App。本政策说明我们收集哪些数据、为什么收集、存在哪里、保存多久，以及你怎样删除它们。

我们收集什么

数据来源用途
Apple 账号标识、Apple 提供的邮箱（可能是 Apple 的隐藏邮箱）和名字「通过 Apple 登录」让你登录，并确认注销时是你本人。Apple 提供的名字只用来预填你的名字。
Google 账号标识、邮箱，以及 Google 提供的基本资料（比如名字）「通过 Google 登录」让你登录，并确认注销时是你本人。Google 提供的名字只用来预填你的名字。
邮箱「用邮箱登录」让你登录。我们只给这个邮箱发送你请求的 6 位验证码：用来登录，或确认注销账号。
名字、用户名、胜利口号、所选角色你填写（名字可能先由 Apple 或 Google 预填，你可以修改）在对决、好友列表和你的主页上显示
挑战内容：标题、各方的承诺、赌注文字、附言，以及对决按哪个时区计日你和对手填写；时区取自提出条件一方的手机进行对决，并在双方的记录里保存
打卡记录和打卡照片你上传证明你完成了当天的承诺，对手可以查看
兑现照片你上传证明战利品已兑现，对手可以查看
私信你发送送达给收件的好友
你给好友起的备注名你填写只给你自己看，也会用在你收到的通知里
好友关系和好友申请你的操作让你和好友互相挑战、查看主页
角色等级与经验你的打卡显示等级
你拥有的角色，以及商店给出的交易编号你在 App 内购买角色时（由 Apple 确认）或恢复购买时解锁角色、恢复购买、处理商店退款
最近活跃时间你使用 App 时让好友看到你最近是否在线
推送设备标识、手机语言、时区你允许通知后发送通知，并用你的语言、按你的时区（夜间静音）发送
隐私和通知设置你的选择决定谁能看你的主页、发哪些通知
战利品图标类别和你的手动图标选择赌注分类及你的操作显示已有图标，并记住你的个人选择
拉黑名单你拉黑别人时让你们互相无法联系
举报：被举报内容的副本、理由和补充说明你举报时让我们处理举报。每条举报的副本会通过邮件发给处理举报的人。

一个邮箱，一个账号： 用 Apple、Google 或邮箱登录时，只要邮箱地址相同，就会进入同一个账号（Apple 的隐藏邮箱不会和其他登录方式匹配）。除了你请求的验证码，以及你来信时我们的回复，我们不会给你发任何邮件。

我们不做的事： 我们不使用广告，不做跨 App 跟踪，不使用第三方统计或分析工具，不出售任何数据。我们不收集你的位置、通讯录或健康数据。照片只在你主动拍摄或选择时上传，上传前会去掉位置和拍摄设备等信息。

只存在你手机上的数据： App 语言选择、挑战草稿、已看过的对决动画，以及战利品排序、拖动位置、显示和筛选设置等，只保存在你的设备上，不会上传。战利品手动图标选择会保存到你的账号。

我们怎样改进 App

我们会以汇总的形式使用我们自己的记录，了解 App 是怎样被使用的，并用来改进它。我们不出售数据，也不为此与他人共享数据。

汇总只看整体数字（比如有多少人完成了第一次打卡），不针对某一个人，也不使用私信或照片的内容。我们不为此使用第三方统计或分析工具；生成汇总报告的程序运行在 GitHub 上，只处理总数。

谁能看到

  • 对手： 能看到你在这场对决里的名字、角色、打卡、照片和兑现记录。
  • 好友： 能看到你的主页，范围由你在设置里的「隐私」决定，包括谁能看、对决记录显示多久以内的。
  • 其他人： 按你的隐私设置。不在可见范围内时，你在别人的主页上会显示为「匿名对手」。
  • 被你拉黑的人： 看不到你的主页，也无法联系你。我们不会通知对方，但对方可能发现某些操作不能用了。
  • 你给好友起的备注名： 只有你自己能看到，包括在你收到的通知里；好友本人看不到。
  • 通知： 通知会显示它涉及的内容，比如私信的文字、承诺、赌注，或你给好友起的备注名，所以可能出现在收到通知的手机的锁屏上。锁屏是否显示内容，由这部手机的通知预览设置决定。
  • 举报： 只有我们处理举报的人能看到；每条举报的副本会通过邮件发给他们（由 Resend 发送、Cloudflare 转发）。被举报的人不会知道是谁举报的。

存在哪里

数据存储在 Supabase 提供的云服务上，服务器位于新加坡（亚马逊云 ap-southeast-1）。照片存在私有存储里，只有对决的双方能读取；不再被任何记录使用的照片（比如被拒绝的兑现照片）从那一刻起对手就无法再打开，并在一两天内清除。

为了运行 Duel，我们使用以下服务商，每家只做列出的工作：

  • Supabase：我们的数据库、照片存储和登录（服务器在新加坡），以及服务器程序。
  • Apple：「通过 Apple 登录」，App 内购买（我们向 Apple 核实每笔角色购买，并接收 Apple 的退款通知），以及送达通知的 Apple 推送服务（APNs）；通知的文字会经过 Apple。
  • Google：「通过 Google 登录」。
  • Resend：把登录和注销验证码发到你的邮箱，并把举报副本发给我们。
  • Cloudflare：接收发到我们 duelhabit.com 地址的邮件（比如你发给我们的邮件和举报副本），并转发到我们自己的邮箱。
  • OpenAI：启用战利品自动图标分类时，我们将新挑战的赌注文字发送给 OpenAI，用来从已有图标类别中选择。请求不附加账号标识、名字、照片或私信，但赌注里你主动写下的内容会随文字发送。分类请求设置为不保存 API 响应。
  • GitHub：托管本政策和使用条款的网页，并运行和保存我们的汇总报告（只有总数）。

除了这些服务商，以及为我们自己的邮箱提供服务的邮件服务商，我们不与其他第三方共享你的数据，除非法律要求。这些服务商可能在你所在的国家或地区以外处理数据，包括新加坡和美国。我们的服务商（比如 Supabase）还会保留技术日志，包括 IP 地址、设备和 App 信息以及登录记录，用来运行服务、保障安全；这些日志只保留有限的时间，我们不会用它们来跟踪你。

保存多久，怎样删除

数据在你的账号存在期间一直保存。举报记录例外：处理完一年后删除；未处理的举报会一直保留到处理完。发给处理举报的人的邮件副本不会自动删除。通知的内容（比如私信的文字）在我们的发送队列里保留约一周，之后删除。你可以随时在 App 的「设置 › 注销账号」中删除账号，删除立即生效、无法恢复：

  • 会删除： 你的名字、用户名、口号、角色和等级，以及你拥有角色的记录（购买本身留在你的 Apple ID 上，新账号可以恢复）；你的邮箱，以及你的 Apple 或 Google 登录关联（对 Apple，我们同时向 Apple 撤销本 App 的授权）；所有私信（双方的），以及发送队列里发给你或提到你的通知；好友和好友申请，以及你给好友、好友给你起的备注名；你上传的打卡和兑现照片（从注销起对手就无法再打开，文件会在一两天内清除）；推送设备记录。提到你的技术日志（比如邮箱和 IP 地址）不会立即删除，由服务商保留有限的时间后清除。
  • 会保留： 你参与过的对决会留在对手的记录里，你显示为「已注销用户」，不带任何个人信息。挑战的标题、承诺、赌注文字和附言属于双方共同的记录，会一并保留。
  • 进行中的对决 算你认输；尚未开始的对决和未处理的挑战会被取消。
  • 举报记录 不随注销删除，按上面的期限删除。如果你的某张照片正被举报、还没处理，它会保留到处理完，之后在一两天内清除。

注销后再次登录，无论用 Apple、Google 还是邮箱，都会创建一个全新的账号。

儿童

Duel 面向 13 岁及以上的用户。我们不会有意收集 13 岁以下儿童的数据。如果你认为有儿童向我们提供了数据，请联系我们，我们会删除。

你的权利

你可以在 App 里查看和修改你的资料、调整隐私和通知设置、注销账号。如需其他协助（比如询问我们保存了哪些数据），请发邮件联系我们。你也可以随时发邮件，反对我们把你的数据用于上面的汇总统计，我们会把你的数据排除在外。

变更

本政策更新时，新版本会发布在这个页面，并修改上方的生效日期。

Duel ("we") is an app for duelling friends on everyday habits. This policy explains what data we collect, why, where it is stored, how long we keep it, and how you delete it.

One email, one account: signing in with Apple, Google or email using the same email address opens the same account (Apple's private relay address never matches another sign-in). Apart from the codes you ask for, and our replies when you write to us, we send you no email.

What we don't do: no advertising, no tracking across apps, no third-party analytics, and we never sell data. We do not collect your location, contacts or health data. Photos are uploaded only when you take or choose one, and without their location or camera details.

Data that stays on your phone: your language choice, Challenge drafts, which Duel animations you have already seen, and Spoils sorting, drag positions, label and filter settings are stored on your device only. Manual Spoils icon selections are saved to your account.

How we improve Duel

We use our own records, in summary form, to see how the app is used and to improve it. We do not sell data or share it for this purpose.

The summaries count totals (for example, how many people finish their first Check-in), never one person, and they never use the contents of messages or photos. We use no third-party statistics or analytics tool for this; the program that writes our summary report runs on GitHub and handles totals only.

Who can see what

  • Your opponent sees your name, Character, check-ins, photos and redemptions in that Duel.
  • Friends see your profile, as far as your Privacy settings allow (who may see it, and how far back your Duel record shows).
  • Others see what your Privacy settings allow. Outside that, you appear on other people's profiles as "Anonymous opponent".
  • People you block can't see your profile or reach you. We don't tell them, though they may notice that some actions stop working.
  • Names you give Friends are seen only by you, also in your notifications; the Friend never sees them.
  • Notifications show what they are about — for example a direct message's text, a commitment, a stake, or the name you gave a Friend — so they can appear on the lock screen of the phone that gets them. That phone's notification preview setting decides whether they do.
  • Reports are seen only by those of us who handle them; a copy of each report is emailed to them (sent through Resend, forwarded by Cloudflare). The person reported is not told who reported them.

Where it is stored

Data is stored with Supabase, on servers in Singapore (Amazon Web Services, ap-southeast-1). Photos are kept in private storage that only the two players of a Duel can read; a photo that no record uses any more (for example a denied redemption's) can no longer be opened by your opponent from that moment, and is removed within a day or two.

To run Duel we use these service providers, each only for the job listed:

  • Supabase: our database, photo storage and sign-in (servers in Singapore), and our server functions.
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple, in-app purchases (we verify each Character purchase with Apple and receive Apple's refund notifications), and the Apple Push Notification service, which delivers notifications; their text passes through Apple.
  • Google: Sign in with Google.
  • Resend: sends sign-in and deletion codes to your email address, and copies of reports to us.
  • Cloudflare: receives mail sent to our duelhabit.com addresses (such as an email you send us, and copies of reports) and forwards it to our own mailboxes.
  • OpenAI: when automatic Spoils icon classification is enabled, we send new Challenges’ Stake text to OpenAI to select an existing icon category. The request does not attach account identifiers, names, photos or direct messages, but anything you include in the Stake text is sent with it. Classification requests disable API response storage.
  • GitHub: hosts the web pages of this policy and the terms, and runs and stores our summary report (totals only).

Apart from these providers, and the email services that host our own mailboxes, we share your data with no other third parties, unless the law requires it. These providers may process data outside your country, including in Singapore and the United States. Our providers, such as Supabase, also keep technical logs, including IP addresses, device and app details, and sign-in events, for a limited time to run and secure the service; we do not use them to track you.

How long we keep it, and how to delete it

We keep your data while your account exists. Reports are the exception: they are deleted a year after they are handled, and an open report is kept until it is handled. The email copies sent to the people who handle reports are not deleted automatically. A notification's content (for example a direct message's text) stays in our sending queue for about a week, and is then deleted. You can delete your account at any time in Settings › Delete account. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone:

  • Deleted: your name, Username, slogan, Characters and levels, and the record of which Characters you own (the purchase itself stays with your Apple ID, and a new account can restore it); your email address and your Apple or Google sign-in link (for Apple, we also revoke the app's authorization with Apple); every direct message, sent and received, and every queued notification to you or naming you; your Friends and Friend requests, and the names you and your Friends gave each other; the check-in and redemption photos you uploaded (your opponent can no longer open them from that moment, and the files are removed within a day or two); your push device records. Technical logs that mention you (such as your email and IP address) are not deleted at once; our providers keep them for a limited time.
  • Kept: Duels you took part in stay in your opponent's history, with you shown as "Deleted account" and nothing personal attached. A Challenge's title, commitments, stakes text and message belong to both players' record and stay with it.
  • Ongoing Duels end as your concession; Duels not yet started and open Challenges are cancelled.
  • Reports are not deleted with your account; they follow the period above. A photo of yours under an open report is kept until that report is handled, and then removed within a day or two.

After deletion, signing in again — with Apple, Google or email — creates a brand-new account.

Children

Duel is for people aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

In the app you can see and edit your profile, change your privacy and notification settings, and delete your account. For anything else — for example, to ask what data we hold — email us. You can also object at any time to your data being used in the summaries above: email us, and we leave your data out.
