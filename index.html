Duel

和朋友在生活习惯上对决：各自定下目标，按天打卡；谁没打卡，谁掉血。先到结算日还站着的一方赢，输的一方兑现赌注。

Duel is a habit game for two friends: each sets a goal, checks in by the day, and loses HP for every miss. Whoever is still standing at settlement wins; the loser pays the stake.
